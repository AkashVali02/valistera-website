WHAT WE CAN SUPPORT

Sterile operations built around contamination control.

Support is tailored to your site, system, product context, risk profile and internal quality procedures.

Sterile manufacturing control depends on the interaction between facility design, people, processes, utilities, environmental monitoring, qualification and operational discipline. These elements need to function as one integrated contamination-control system.

We support assessment, documentation and qualification activities that connect contamination risks to preventive controls, objective evidence and continued verification across the sterile-manufacturing lifecycle.

01

Contamination Control Strategy (CCS) Support

Support development and review of CCS elements, interfaces, supporting evidence and action tracking.

02

EU GMP Annex 1 Readiness

Gap assessment and readiness support against Annex 1 expectations for sterile operations and contamination control.

03

Cleanroom & HVAC Qualification

Qualification support for classification, airflow, HEPA integrity, pressure cascades, recovery and environmental conditions.

04

Aseptic Process Simulation Documentation Support

Protocol, report and evidence support for APS/media-fill design, execution, interventions, deviations and review.

05

Sterilisation & Depyrogenation System Readiness

Readiness and qualification support for sterilizers, depyrogenation tunnels, cycles, loads and critical parameters.

06

Environmental Monitoring Programme Review

Review of EM strategy, locations, frequencies, limits, trends, investigations and linkage to contamination-control principles.

07

Cleanroom Zoning & Personnel/Material Flow Review

Review zoning, personnel and material flows, transfer controls and contamination risks across sterile areas.

08

Sterile Utility Assessment & Action Tracking

Assessment and action tracking for critical sterile utilities, process gases and associated monitoring or control systems.

CONTAMINATION-CONTROL LIFECYCLE

From contamination risk to continued state of control.

Annex 1 places contamination control at the centre of sterile manufacturing. Our approach connects risk assessment, facility and process controls, qualification, APS, monitoring and continued verification.

01

Map contamination risk

Identify contamination pathways, critical process steps, personnel and material interfaces, utilities, interventions and environmental risks.

02

Structure the CCS

Connect contamination risks with preventive and detective controls, responsibilities, supporting evidence and improvement actions.

03

Control facility & flow

Review cleanroom zoning, HVAC performance, pressure relationships, personnel/material movement and transfer controls.

04

Qualify systems & utilities

Support qualification and evidence review for cleanrooms, HVAC, sterilisation systems, depyrogenation equipment and critical utilities.

05

Challenge the aseptic process

Support APS/media-fill strategy, intervention assessment, execution documentation, deviations and evaluation of aseptic-process performance.

06

Monitor & verify

Use environmental, utility, process and qualification evidence to identify trends, investigate loss of control and support continued verification.
