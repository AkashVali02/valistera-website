GMP Manufacturing Operations and In-Process Controls

Lesson 1.5 · Narrated learning · Optional captions and key explanations

Technical clarification for this recording: Independent dispensing checks are not optional merely because a local risk assessment prefers otherwise. EU GMP Chapter 5, 5.37–5.38 addresses controlled dispensing and a recorded independent check of each dispensed material and its weight or volume. US 21 CFR 211.101(c)–(d) sets second-person checks with defined automated-equipment exceptions under 211.68.

Dispensing and verification — jurisdiction-specific requirements

For US finished pharmaceutical manufacturing, 21 CFR 211.101(c) requires a second-person examination of each dispensed component container for release status, correct weight or measure, and proper identification, with a specified exception for weighing, measuring or subdividing performed by automated equipment under 21 CFR 211.68. Under 21 CFR 211.101(d), component addition requires second-person verification for manual addition; a defined single-person verification applies when the operation is performed by qualifying automated equipment.

Other jurisdictions and product categories may differ. Follow applicable regulations and approved procedures rather than treating every independent check as optional based only on site risk.
