Installation Qualification (IQ)

Continue the V-201 case

Inspect the fictional V-201 oral-liquid vessel against a controlled installation baseline. No aseptic process, site-specific acceptance criteria or actual qualification records are represented.

Prerequisite: Module 3 — Design Qualification.

Learning time and format

19.1 minutes of narration. Allow approximately 4–5 hours including 200 minutes of suggested practice, reading and assessment. These are unmeasured planning estimates, not accredited contact time.

Core explanations and worked reasoning are narrated. Reference tables restate the examples; activities and model answers are reading exercises.
