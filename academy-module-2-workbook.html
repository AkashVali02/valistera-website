Capstone self-review rubric

Score each dimension 0 (missing), 1 (partly supported), or 2 (clear and supported): intended-use scope; requirement quality; evidence traceability; risk/control rationale; decision and open actions. A maximum of 10 is a learning rubric, not a competency certification.

A decision that invents missing evidence or approves routine use despite unresolved critical gaps needs correction regardless of total score.
