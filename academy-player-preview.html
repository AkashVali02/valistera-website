Further reading: Where GMP requirements come from

GMP requirements depend on the product, manufacturing activity and market. Important reference frameworks include US FDA 21 CFR Parts 210 and 211, European Union EudraLex Volume 4, WHO GMP guidance, PIC/S GMP guidance and India's Drugs Rules including Schedule M. This module focuses on human medicinal products; veterinary and other product categories may have different legal frameworks. These sources are not interchangeable: a manufacturer must identify the requirements applicable to its licensed operations and destination markets.

This introductory module teaches broadly shared GMP principles. It does not replace current jurisdiction-specific regulations, approved site procedures or qualified professional assessment.

Data integrity: ALCOA stands for Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original and Accurate. Additional ALCOA+ principles include Complete, Consistent, Enduring and Available. “Retained” is a practical record-control concept, not the “O” in ALCOA.
