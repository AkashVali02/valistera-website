Scope and limitations

Browser tests cover 320/390/768/1280 widths, automated axe checks, skip links, keyboard Enter disclosure/play activation, collapsed images and descriptions, native timeline clicks, captions, assessment boundaries, optional storage and failure/retry paths. Media and frame checks measure encoding, caption/source timing and correspondence to PNGs. They do not establish human clarity, pronunciation, physical-device behavior or regulatory compliance.

Manual audiovisual review, physical Android/iOS, screen-reader and zoom acceptance, rights/privacy acceptance, independent GMP consultant sign-off and explicit owner production authorization remain required.
