REGULATORY DOCUMENTATION WORKFLOW

From requirement mapping to lifecycle-ready documentation.

Regulatory quality is built through connected source evidence, controlled technical content and disciplined review. Our workflow helps convert manufacturing and quality information into coherent regulatory documentation.

01

Map requirements

Identify applicable dossier expectations, market-specific requirements, lifecycle stage, document ownership and evidence needs.

02

Establish source evidence

Identify controlled source documents and confirm that manufacturing, analytical, validation and specification information is available.

03

Coordinate CMC content

Align technical content across functions, versions and dependencies so the quality dossier remains internally consistent.

04

Quality review

Review completeness, technical consistency, terminology, traceability and alignment between dossier content and underlying evidence.

05

Submission readiness

Confirm outstanding actions, document status, cross-references and review readiness before client approval or submission activity.

06

Respond & maintain

Support authority-response packages, regulatory commitments, post-approval changes and ongoing lifecycle documentation.
