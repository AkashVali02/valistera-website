Sources, examples and provenance

Technical basis

Rechecked against official sources on 27 October 2026. This module focuses on human-medicinal-product qualification planning using EU GMP and ICH principles, with WHO validation guidance as supplementary context. It does not claim complete Indian, US, veterinary or product-specific compliance coverage.

  • EU GMP Annex 15 (2015), §§1–4 and 11
  • ICH Q9(R1), §§3–5, 7 and Annex I.2–I.3
  • WHO TRS 1019 Annex 3 (2019), §§5–10 and 15

The European Commission listing still identifies the 2015 Annex 15 as the operative text. The EMA 2026 Annex 15 concept-paper consultation is closed; its July corrigendum updates the revision timetable, not the operative GMP text. Revision proposals are not taught as adopted requirements. The EMA listing identifies Q9(R1) as effective from 26 July 2023, with the current corrected publication listed in 2025.

How to distinguish the material

Regulatory principles are linked to the sources above. V-201, document IDs, responsibilities, diagrams, questions and the dataset are original fictional teaching examples. The three-level score, 0.5 °C example limit and 80% quiz target are not regulatory acceptance criteria.

The recorded narration is transcribed with conventional written abbreviations and numerals on each lesson page. Required reading adds assessed material that is not in the recordings. Activities and model reasoning are explicitly separate extensions. Diagrams have nearby text labels, explanatory narration and full-size versions; the data chart has an exact-value table.
