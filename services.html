LOCATION & DELIVERY

Headquartered in Pune.
Remote services available worldwide.

With our headquarters in Pune, Maharashtra, India, we offer remote technical collaboration to pharmaceutical manufacturers and consulting teams worldwide. Each engagement is assessed against its technical scope, applicable requirements, available evidence and delivery needs.

International remote collaboration

Remote document review, qualification planning, gap assessments, regulatory CMC coordination and training can be scoped for clients worldwide. International projects are planned around the target market, applicable requirements, working language, time-zone coordination and secure document exchange.

Work that needs site attendance

Physical inspections, witnessing and execution activities may need an on-site arrangement. Location, specialist availability, access requirements, travel and commercial terms are confirmed in the proposal before any site work is committed.

Projects involving more than one facility

Tell us which locations and systems are involved and where documentation or processes differ. A common planning framework can be proposed, with site-specific evidence and approval responsibilities defined for each facility.

HOW WE ENGAGE

Defined scope.
Usable outcomes.

Each engagement begins by clarifying the objective, applicable standards, current state, available evidence, stakeholders and expected deliverables. We then agree the scope, review approach, assumptions, responsibilities and timelines.

Outputs may include gap registers, review comments, action trackers, requirements matrices, document structures, training material or draft documentation for client review. Final approval and regulated decisions remain with the client's authorised personnel.

QUALITY & COMPLIANCE

Compliance-minded. Evidence-led.

Work is scoped against applicable client procedures, regulatory requirements and guidance. Depending on the engagement, reference frameworks may include GxP, FDA 21 CFR requirements, EU GMP Annexes 1, 11 & 15, ICH Q9(R1), and ISO 9001:2015 quality-management principles.
