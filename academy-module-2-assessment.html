Module 02 / Final self-assessment

Test the decisions, not just the terms

20 questions · Suggested practice target: 80% (16/20). Review explanations and retry as needed.

This is a learning target, not a regulatory threshold or formal competency assessment. Unanswered questions must be completed before scoring. Responses are not sent to a server. This is an open-book self-study exercise: answers and explanations are visible in the page source and downloadable key. Scores do not demonstrate independent competence. Read the lesson notes and required extensions where provided before attempting it.

Printable answer key and explanations

1. Agree intended use and justify the required operating range — A supplier capability or another project does not establish this process need.

2. Link each system to status, evidence and open actions — Decision makers need evidence-linked status and remaining work.

3. A utility interface owner and relevant qualification evidence — Define interface dependencies and responsibilities rather than leaving a boundary gap.

4. At defined reference points, indication error must be within ±0.5 °C — The last option is measurable, once the fictional basis, points and method are approved; ±0.5 °C is not a general GMP limit.

5. Keep it open while applicability and affected tests are assessed — A changed measurement chain needs an applicability assessment and affected verification.

6. A patient receives an incorrect dose with an adverse effect — Distinguish patient harm from equipment failure and detection failure.

7. Examine severity, evidence and controls before deciding — An ordinal product can conceal important differences.

8. A challenge of the shared fault and resulting response — Functional testing must address the shared dependency, not merely documentation or configuration.

9. It exceeds the limit because its magnitude is 0.6 °C — Use absolute magnitude for a two-sided error limit; apply the approved uncertainty/decision rule in real testing.

10. Assess impact on that simulation and obtain stage authorization — Conditional progression concerns a specific activity and requires justified impact assessment and authorization.

11. Document the URS change impact and reassess affected DQ evidence before relying on its status — A change to user requirements can affect the design qualification basis.

12. Assess interface compatibility and affected verification — Qualification of a supplying system does not establish its suitability for every receiving interface.

13. Pending justification and approval of the acceptance basis — Teaching assumptions cannot replace authorized project requirements.

14. Assess the firmware change, applicability of supplier evidence and affected site tests — Supplier results require documented applicability to the installed configuration.

15. Control effectiveness has not been demonstrated to support the revised score — Residual risk scoring should follow evidence of effectiveness, not an untested proposal.

16. Challenge primary-sensor failure and verify independent detection and transfer inhibition — The verification should challenge the common-cause scenario and protective response.

17. +0.31 °C — The signed arithmetic mean is +3.1/10 = +0.31 °C.

18. One — Only 0.6 °C exceeds the illustrative limit; 0.5 °C equals it.

19. Review intended use, requirements, risks and affected verification evidence — New intended use may affect linked requirements and qualification evidence.

20. Completion of an educational exercise, not independent competence — Formal competency and authorization require an applicable independently approved process.
