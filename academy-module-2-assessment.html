Test the decisions, not just the terms

20 questions · Suggested practice target: 80% (16/20). Review explanations and retry as needed.

This is a learning target, not a regulatory threshold or formal competency assessment. Unanswered questions must be completed before scoring. Responses are not sent to a server. This is an open-book self-study exercise: answers and explanations are visible in the page source and downloadable key. Scores do not demonstrate independent competence. Printable answer key and explanations

1. Agree intended use and justify the required operating range — A supplier capability or another project does not establish this process need.

2. Link each system to status, evidence and open actions — Decision makers need evidence-linked status and remaining work.

3. A utility interface owner and relevant qualification evidence — Define interface dependencies and responsibilities rather than leaving a boundary gap.

4. At defined reference points, indication error must be within ±0.5 °C — The last option is measurable, once the fictional basis, points and method are approved; ±0.5 °C is not a general GMP limit.

5. Keep it open while applicability and affected tests are assessed — A changed measurement chain needs an applicability assessment and affected verification.

6. A patient receives an incorrect dose with an adverse effect — Distinguish patient harm from equipment failure and detection failure.

7. Examine severity, evidence and controls before deciding — An ordinal product can conceal important differences.

8. A challenge of the shared fault and resulting response — Functional testing must address the shared dependency, not merely documentation or configuration.

9. It exceeds the limit because its magnitude is 0.6 °C — Use absolute magnitude for a two-sided error limit; apply the approved uncertainty/decision rule in real testing.

10. Assess impact on that simulation and obtain stage authorization — Conditional progression concerns a specific activity and requires justified impact assessment and authorization.

11. Design review pending, with owner and document reference — A specific status and evidence reference expose remaining work.

12. An interface owner and relevant utility evidence — The boundary must not create a gap in responsibility or evidence.

13. A temperature tolerance awaiting process justification — Missing process justification must be resolved; the statement is not ready for acceptance testing.

14. No; assess applicability and integrity — Configuration and transport/installation effects can change applicability.

15. Harder detection before the consequence — This teaching scale uses higher values for greater detection difficulty; scales must be defined.

16. A possible common-cause failure — A shared dependency may undermine claimed independence.

17. 0.31 °C — Their sum is 3.1 °C; divided by ten gives 0.31 °C.

18. One — Only 0.6 °C exceeds the illustrative limit; 0.5 equals it.

19. Assess scope, requirements, risks and affected evidence — The new intended use may affect several linked documents and controls.

20. Self-study completion only — Formal competency and authorization require the applicable independent/site process.
