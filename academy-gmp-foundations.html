Pharmaceutical GMP Foundations

Build a practical understanding of Good Manufacturing Practice and how controlled operations, people, equipment and documentation support medicine quality and patient safety.

Beginner-friendlySelf-pacedFree accessLesson 1.1 available

What you'll learn

  • Explain why GMP is essential to pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • Identify the roles of personnel, premises, equipment and documentation.
  • Recognise the relationship between GMP, quality systems and risk management.
  • Apply GMP thinking to a practical equipment-readiness scenario.

Who this is for

Pharmaceutical students, early-career quality professionals, production and engineering personnel, and anyone building foundational GMP knowledge.

Educational content only. Not a substitute for site-specific training, approved procedures or formal competency assessment.
