Apply the IQ reasoning

20 distinct application questions. This open-book self-study assessment exposes its answer key in the source and below. The suggested practice target is 85% (17/20), not a regulatory threshold, IQ acceptance rule or proof of independent competence. Unanswered questions must be completed before scoring. Answers are not sent to a server.

1. A team verified the installed vessel against its accepted arrangement. Which conclusion is supported?

2. Two inspectors used different revisions of the instrument schedule. What should happen?

3. An equipment tag matches but the manufacturer serial differs from the schedule. What is justified?

4. A drawing lacks the required removal clearance. How should a measured 300 mm be treated?

5. A gasket certificate describes a lot that cannot be linked to the installed gasket. What remains open?

6. The installed material matches its specification. Which claim still needs separate evidence?

7. A utility line is labelled correctly but connected to a different branch than the drawing specifies. What is appropriate?

8. A utility was qualified before a new vessel branch was added. What is needed for the extension?

9. Indication is 40.4 °C and reference is 40.0 °C. Which signed error and simple comparison are correct?

10. A current certificate has suitable range but identifies another instrument. What follows?

11. An instrument is found outside its required calibration status after IQ measurements. What should be assessed?

12. The control-system version is recorded during IQ. Which next step is still needed as applicable?

13. A supplier manual covers a different installed model. What is the strongest response?

14. A factory test is proposed for reuse after transport and site assembly. What supports reuse?

15. A photograph shows a readable nameplate but cannot be linked to any location or check. What is needed?

16. A serial number was transposed in a handwritten record. Which correction is appropriate?

17. A protocol check was not performed because access was unsafe. Which status is accurate?

18. An installed valve differs from the accepted specification. Which route is defensible?

19. Nine of ten checks are supported, but gasket traceability remains open. What does 90% mean?

20. A proposed dry OQ activity is unaffected by an open wetted-system finding. What supports conditional progression?

Answer key and explanations

1. The installed arrangement matches the reviewed scope — Limit the conclusion to installation evidence; performance and cleaning need their own studies.

2. Reconcile the controlled baseline and assess the affected checks — A consistent applicable baseline is needed to interpret the observations.

3. Resolve the identity discrepancy before relying on the match — A tag match does not resolve conflicting equipment identity.

4. As an observation whose acceptance criterion remains unresolved — The observed distance cannot create its own criterion; no universal 300 mm limit is taught.

5. Traceability between the material evidence and installed item — The missing item-to-evidence link must be resolved through an applicable controlled approach.

6. The cleaning process is effective for the intended residues — Matching material identity does not demonstrate cleaning effectiveness.

7. Record and investigate the connection discrepancy — A label does not correct an incorrect or unassessed physical connection.

8. Assessment of the change and applicable verification scope — The extension must be evaluated; previous qualification does not automatically cover a changed system.

9. +0.4 °C; within the fictional ±0.5 °C limit — Indication minus reference is +0.4 °C. This arithmetic is not a complete uncertainty or acceptance decision.

10. Resolve certificate applicability to the installed instrument — Identity, not just date and range, is necessary for applicability.

11. The validity of affected measurements and required follow-up — A new calibration record alone does not establish the validity of earlier evidence.

12. Validation of relevant functions and controls under the approved approach — Version identification does not demonstrate functional controls or complete Annex 11 compliance.

13. Verify applicability and obtain the required correct instructions — The handover needs instructions relevant to the installed model/configuration.

14. Applicable controlled evidence and justified lack of relevant impact — Assess configuration, method, data and effects of transport/installation; retain required site checks.

15. Context and an evidence reference connecting it to the inspected item — An image needs a clear relationship to the relevant equipment and verification record.

16. Preserve the original and make an attributed, dated correction — Follow the approved procedure, preserve the original information and record a reason where appropriate.

17. Not checked, with the limitation and follow-up recorded — An incomplete check is distinct from a justified scope exclusion or a demonstrated pass.

18. Document the discrepancy, assess impact and authorize correction or change — A discrepancy requires an evidence-based controlled response and defined verification.

19. Numerical coverage only; it does not authorize the next stage — 9/10 is 90%, but significance and activity-specific assessment govern the decision.

20. Documented no-significant-impact assessment and responsible authorization — Annex 15 §2.10 concerns a defined next-stage activity; the open finding is not automatically closed.
