Supplementary reading: Packaging and label control

Core controls: Packaging operations should prevent product, strength, batch and label mix-ups through approved instructions, line clearance, segregation, correct material issuance, reconciliation where required and documented checks. Obsolete or superseded printed materials must be controlled to prevent accidental use.

Practical example: A wrong-strength carton found on a line is a potential mix-up signal. Secure the operation as required, preserve the carton and records, notify the responsible quality function, and assess whether other packs or materials may be affected before authorized restart.

Regulatory context: US 21 CFR 211.122 concerns packaging and labeling material controls; 211.125 covers labeling issuance; 211.130 covers packaging and labeling operations. EU GMP Volume 4 Chapter 5 addresses production controls, including packaging operations. Site procedures must define the applicable checks.
