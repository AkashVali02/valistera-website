Apply the OQ reasoning

20 distinct application questions. This open-book self-study assessment exposes its answer key in the source and below. The suggested practice target is 85% (17/20), not a regulatory threshold, OQ acceptance rule or proof of independent competence. Unanswered questions must be completed before scoring. Answers are not sent to a server.

1. A temperature alarm test passes. Which claim still needs separate evidence?

2. A material finding remains open at IQ handover. What determines whether a proposed OQ activity can proceed?

3. A supplier protocol tests a different software configuration. What is needed before use?

4. A test has no defined response-time criterion. What is appropriate?

5. An input occurs at 14:20:10.0 and the defined output at 14:20:13.2. What is the elapsed time?

6. The intended range is inside the manufacturer design envelope. What does this establish by itself?

7. A plan requires all pairs of two loads and three speeds. How many combinations does that plan contain?

8. Which rationale best supports a selected low-load challenge?

9. An alarm annunciates, but the required output does not change. How should the combined requirement be evaluated?

10. A simulated transmitter signal is injected downstream of the sensing element. Which limitation should be stated?

11. The fictional design requires manual reset after a trip. What should recovery verification include?

12. An error is +0.4 °C against a simple ±0.5 °C limit. What is the limited conclusion?

13. A stated teaching rule requires absolute error plus expanded uncertainty ≤0.5 °C. Error is +0.4 °C and uncertainty 0.2 °C. What follows?

14. A control trend is satisfactory for one selected minute but unstable during the prescribed evaluation interval. What is defensible?

15. After a simulated interruption, the machine runs again but a setting is lost. What does this indicate?

16. A report contains selected screenshots of a raw electronic trend. What should happen to the applicable original data?

17. A logging gap occurs during a challenge. Which response preserves defensible evidence?

18. A parameter correction is followed by a passing retest. Which statement is justified?

19. Eleven of twelve tests pass, with one protective-function failure. What does approximately 91.7% mean?

20. OQ supports finalizing an operating and cleaning procedure. Which additional conclusion would be unsupported?

Answer key and explanations

1. The product blend is uniform throughout — A defined alarm response does not establish product mixing performance.

2. Documented impact and authorization for that activity — Assess the specific next activity and obtain authorization; progress counts and promises do not establish readiness.

3. Review applicability and supplement or revise appropriately — The site establishes suitability for its actual configuration and procedures before approval.

4. Resolve the criterion through the controlled process — Acceptance cannot be invented from the result. Define and approve the test basis before deciding acceptance.

5. 3.2 seconds — Subtract synchronized start and end times: 13.2 minus 10.0 equals 3.2 seconds. Clock suitability remains necessary.

6. Only that it fits the stated design envelope — A design rating is not evidence of qualification coverage; tests and rationale are still needed.

7. Six combinations — Two times three equals six. This is the stated teaching matrix, not a regulatory minimum number of tests.

8. It targets an identified immersion or control risk — A low-load condition needs a mechanism-specific rationale; it is not universally worst case.

9. Fail and investigate the missing required response — The complete stated requirement includes the output response; annunciation alone is insufficient.

10. The sensor itself was not challenged by that simulation — The injection boundary excludes upstream sensing; other observations determine whether the actuator was actually checked.

11. The specified reset and controlled restart behavior — Removing the fault and satisfying the required reset/restart sequence are distinct observations.

12. The absolute error meets that simple comparison — The simple comparison passes, but uncertainty, method suitability and the prescribed decision rule remain relevant.

13. Do not accept: the rule gives 0.6 °C — 0.4 + 0.2 = 0.6 °C, above 0.5 °C. This conservative illustrative rule is not a universal GMP formula.

14. Evaluate the complete prescribed interval and investigate — The predefined evaluation and complete original data govern interpretation; selective reporting hides relevant behavior.

15. Recovery needs assessment against its full requirements — Running again does not establish correct retained configuration or data continuity.

16. Retain it under defined controls and retention arrangements — Summaries do not replace applicable original records. Retention depends on the record and applicable obligations.

17. Retain partial data, assess impact and justify any repeat — Preserve the original and document the gap, investigation and authorized continuation or repeat.

18. The repeat supports only its defined post-change scope — Keep the original failure and assess wider impact; one passing repeat does not establish every related function.

19. A pass rate, not qualification acceptance — 11 divided by 12 is about 91.7%. The significance of the failed test remains; the course practice score is unrelated.

20. Cleaning effectiveness has therefore been validated — A finalized procedure alone does not establish validated cleaning effectiveness or product performance.
