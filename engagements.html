QUALIFICATION & VALIDATION

Sterile facility qualification and lifecycle readiness

Challenge

Qualification activities across equipment, utilities and cleanroom systems required stronger lifecycle structure, traceability and alignment between technical evidence and GMP expectations.

ValiStera approach

Established a risk-based qualification framework connecting intended use, criticality, document hierarchy, protocol strategy, traceability and remediation control.

Typical deliverables
  • Qualification strategy and gap assessment
  • DQ / IQ / OQ / PQ documentation support
  • Requirements and traceability review
  • Deviation and remediation tracking
  • Validated-state lifecycle recommendations
Outcome focus Clearer qualification evidence, stronger lifecycle control and improved inspection readiness.
