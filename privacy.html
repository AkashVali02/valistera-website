LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

ValiStera Solutions respects the privacy of website visitors, prospective clients, training participants and professional contacts. This policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with our website or contact us.

1. Information you provide

You may choose to provide information such as your name, organization, professional contact details, enquiry information, training interests or project-related information when contacting ValiStera.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss potential engagements, provide requested resources, coordinate training, maintain professional correspondence and support legitimate business administration.

3. Confidential information

Please avoid sending highly confidential, proprietary, patient-identifiable or commercially sensitive information through general website or email enquiries unless an appropriate confidentiality arrangement and secure communication method have been established.

4. Website and technical information

Standard technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or infrastructure providers as necessary to operate, secure and maintain the website. The extent of such processing depends on the technologies enabled on the website at the relevant time.

5. Google Analytics, cookies and consent

ValiStera may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the website and to improve website content, performance and services.

Google Analytics is optional on this website. Analytics is not loaded unless you choose Accept analytics through the cookie preference notice. If you reject non-essential cookies, analytics storage remains disabled.

When analytics is accepted, Google Analytics may use cookies such as _ga and related _ga_* cookies to distinguish visitors and measure website usage. We do not enable advertising storage, advertising user-data storage or ad-personalisation through this implementation.

Your analytics preference is stored locally in your browser so the website can remember your choice. You can review or change your choice later using the Cookie settings control available on the website.

Analytics information is processed using Google services and is subject to Google's applicable privacy and data-processing terms. ValiStera uses this information for website measurement and improvement.

6. Third-party links

The website may contain links to third-party platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram or WhatsApp. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and ValiStera does not control those external services.

7. Retention and protection

Business correspondence and enquiry information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, legitimate business needs, contractual obligations or applicable legal requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information under ValiStera's control.

8. Your requests

You may contact ValiStera regarding access, correction or other privacy-related requests concerning personal information held by us, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

9. Contact

Privacy-related enquiries may be sent to contact@valistera.com.

This policy may be updated as ValiStera's services, website functionality or applicable requirements evolve.
