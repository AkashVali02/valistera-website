Further GMP quality-system topics

Foundational GMP includes supplier oversight, complaint handling, recalls, periodic product quality review, cross-contamination prevention and distinct QA and QC responsibilities. This introductory course identifies these as topics for further study rather than claiming comprehensive coverage.
