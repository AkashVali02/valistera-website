## Cross-module controls

- Native WebVTT cue overlap fixed in 10 tracks; embedded cue timestamps also normalized.
- Supplementary text was added after video production. It is **not** represented as narrated or visually illustrated.
- Automated tests do not prove learning validity, audio fidelity, legal compliance or human approval.
