The official index lists the 2015 Annex 15; a revision concept paper is not an adopted replacement. All V-201 designs, revisions, acceptance values, calculation bounds and review decisions are original fictional exercises. The ±0.5 °C example is not an official tolerance; 80% is not a DQ acceptance rule. Measurement-chain arithmetic is a conservative sum, not a complete uncertainty budget. The module flags but does not teach complete CSV, aseptic design or jurisdiction-specific legal compliance.
