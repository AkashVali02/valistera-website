# Module 1 — official-source cross-check

Checked 26 October 2026. This is an AI-assisted educational content check, not a legal opinion, independent GMP review or regulatory approval. Scope: the cited US finished-pharmaceutical and EU human-medicinal-product provisions. WHO, PIC/S and Indian Schedule M are framework signposts only; no detailed current Indian implementation or veterinary compliance determination is made.

The [European Commission current index](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/eudralex/eudralex-volume-4_en) lists Annex 15 effective 1 October 2015, Annex 11 January 2011, and Annex 1 fully applicable since 25 August 2024. A consultation/concept paper does not replace an operative text. The index now distinguishes veterinary rules applying from 16 July 2026; generic references must not imply identical legal applicability across products. Search-result PDF titles sometimes retain “draft” wording; operative status was checked against the Commission index, not inferred from PDF metadata.
