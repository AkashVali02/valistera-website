WHAT WE CAN SUPPORT

Structured work.
Defensible evidence.

Support is tailored to your site, system, product context, risk profile and internal quality procedures.

Build a risk-based foundation for compliant digital systems and automation. Our work emphasises clear scope, traceability, documented rationale, and practical action closure. Activities are agreed to match the maturity and needs of each organisation.

Possible deliverables can include assessment summaries, review comments, requirement-to-evidence matrices, action trackers, draft documents, training material and readiness reports, subject to the agreed scope and client approval.

01

Computerized System Inventory & GxP Impact Assessment

Risk-based system inventory and GxP impact assessment to determine validation, data-integrity and lifecycle controls.

02

Validation Planning

Validation strategy and planning based on intended use, system risk, supplier evidence and applicable requirements.

03

User Requirements & Traceability

Risk-focused user requirements and end-to-end traceability from intended use through testing and release evidence.

04

Supplier Assessment

Supplier and service-provider assessment covering quality systems, SDLC, support, security and relevant compliance evidence.

05

Risk Assessment

Documented assessment focused on patient safety, product quality, data integrity and intended use.

06

Test-Script & Evidence Review

Review of test design, expected results, deviations and objective evidence for adequacy and traceability.

07

Access & Audit-Trail Control Review

Review of user roles, privileged access, segregation, audit-trail configuration and review expectations.

08

Data Migration & Backup/Restore Test Planning

Risk-based planning for migration verification, backup/restore testing, reconciliation and supporting evidence.

09

Periodic Review

Periodic review of validated state, changes, incidents, access, audit trails, performance and supplier status.

10

GAMP 5 / Part 11 / Annex 11 Alignment

Assessment and validation approach aligned, as applicable, with GAMP 5, FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11.
