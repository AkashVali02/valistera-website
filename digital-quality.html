DIGITAL VALIDATION LIFECYCLE

From intended use to continued validated state.

Digital validation is strongest when requirements, risk, supplier evidence, testing and operational controls remain connected throughout the system lifecycle.

01

Define intended use

Establish business purpose, users, records, interfaces, process context and functions that support regulated activity.

02

Assess GxP impact

Determine whether the system can affect patient safety, product quality, data integrity or regulated records and decisions.

03

Risk & criticality

Focus assurance effort on critical functions, failure scenarios, controls and data with the greatest GxP significance.

04

Requirements & traceability

Define risk-focused user requirements and connect critical requirements to specifications, controls and verification evidence.

05

Supplier & design evidence

Assess supplier capability and determine which existing development, configuration and test evidence can be leveraged appropriately.

06

Test & challenge

Verify critical functions and controls using proportionate testing, objective evidence, deviation management and traceable results.

07

Release & operate

Confirm validation evidence, access controls, procedures, training, backup, support and residual actions before operational release.

08

Maintain & review

Preserve the validated state through change control, incident review, access governance, periodic review and supplier oversight.
